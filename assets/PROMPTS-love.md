**Aesthetic:** standing inside a dark intimate room filled with living light — TeamLab / borderless museum immersion. No people, no faces, no text, no logos, no cartoon hearts. Locked camera. High contrast into pure black. Soft large shapes for a tiny projector. Rose / peach / candle-warm / soft violet only.

**Procedural Love (no file needed):** parallax haze volumes, luminous liquid folds, candle-warm breathing core, floating garden of light, heartbeat petals, rising orbs, subtle film grain.

## Tips

- Prefer **darker** frames so live waves stay the hero; pure black edges help tiny projectors.
- Emphasize **seamless loop** / **no cuts** / **locked camera** in Grok Imagine.
- Mute the clip — audio comes from the therapy session.
- Filename must be exactly: `love.mp4`, `love.webm`, `love.jpg`, `love.png`, or `love.webp` (first match wins, that order).
- Demo without a file still works — Love is fully procedural (volumes + caustics + candle + garden + heartbeat).
